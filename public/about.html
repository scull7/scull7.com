About Nathan Sculli

Nathan Sculli is a builder-leader based in Las Vegas, Nevada. He is Director of Engineering at TensorWave, where he is the primary individual contributor on Relay, the control plane behind the largest AMD-based GPU clouds. This page is the prose counterpart to the interactive resume on the homepage.

What this site is

scull7.com is Nathan’s personal resume site. It publishes his background, work history, open-source projects, and contact facts so that recruiters, collaborators, and software agents can read the same record. The homepage is a vim-inspired reader over resume.json. It is not a product company, a hosted API, or a SaaS console.

Background

Twenty years of shipping systems and leading engineers across AI infrastructure, financial services, data platforms, manufacturing, and SaaS. Over a career he has scaled organizations of more than one hundred engineers while remaining an individual contributor on the hard problems — Rust, OCaml, ReasonML, Haskell, and JavaScript.

He has been founder or CTO of three ventures, including Sub Zero Corp. Earlier roles include Banner, Backtrace.io, Marker Trax, Vice President of Engineering at Influential, and engineering leadership at Allegiant Air. Public profiles live on GitHub, LinkedIn, and crates.io.
