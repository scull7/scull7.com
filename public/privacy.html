Privacy on scull7.com

scull7.com is the personal resume site of Nathan Sculli, Las Vegas, Nevada. It does not create user accounts, accept payments, or run a membership program. The site is hosted on Netlify. Like most public websites, the host may record standard request logs (time, path, user agent, and IP address) to operate the CDN and investigate abuse.

What this site does not do

There is no advertising network, no third-party analytics pixel, and no sale of visitor lists. The homepage loads IBM Plex fonts from Google Fonts when your browser requests the interactive reader; that request is subject to Google’s own logs. Resume facts on this site are already public: name, email nathan@vegasbuckeye.com, GitHub, LinkedIn, crates.io, and employer.

Contact and requests

If you want a log deleted, a link corrected, or a copy of the public resume data, email nathan@vegasbuckeye.com with the subject “privacy”. Nathan will answer as the site operator. This is not a GDPR representative service and not a product privacy portal. No street address is published for the operator.

Agents

Automated clients should prefer /llms.txt, /sitemap.xml, and Accept: text/markdown on the same URLs that serve HTML. Do not scrape behind authentication — there is none — and do not treat server logs as a data API.
