## why
Discoverability only counts if a crawler without JavaScript sees the facts, and if a resume edit cannot leave stale HTML.

## scope_in
- Prove Vite preview GET `/` (no JS) of the production-build artifact contains, **in the designated fragment region** (`<noscript id="crawlable-resume">`, excluding JSON-LD and meta/OG), the name, a summary sentence, `TensorWave`, and a real `<a href>` that is not already in Person JSON-LD.
- Prove that changing `public/resume.json` and re-running the documented build updates the crawlable fragment without hand-editing HTML.
- Three **separate** local fixture runs (mutation, empty-URL, missing-job) with restore of committed `public/resume.json` between runs, so the generator cannot hard-code TensorWave/Banner hrefs or leftover job copy.
- Prove unreadable/invalid `resume.json` makes the documented inject/build exit non-zero (no silent stale/empty fragment).
- Keep title / OG / JSON-LD identity strings unchanged.

## scope_out
- Implementing crawler plumbing (T-11) or the inject mechanism itself (T-12), except as consumed by these proofs.
- Headline / OG / JSON-LD rewrite; hero/card chrome; blog; SSR; PDF; GHA; elm-test; new third-party deps.
- Restoring `nathansculli.com` (T-8). Deploying to production DNS. Changing Elm visual design.
- Inventing MW/rack/GPU counts or unpublished TensorWave internals.
